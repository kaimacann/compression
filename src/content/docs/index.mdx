## Assessment snapshot

- <AssessmentBadge stage="submission" label="Due date" /> **1 November by
  23:59**.
- <AssessmentBadge stage="submission" label="Deliverables" /> Submit source
  files through Ed and one PDF report through Canvas.
- <AssessmentBadge stage="submission" label="Page limit" /> The report is

  appendices.
